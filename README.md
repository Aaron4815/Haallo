# Physical AI Data Germany

**Partner data room · Working title · Münster, Germany**

Physical AI Data Germany is an early-stage B2B venture building **task-specific, calibrated and legally documented training data for robotics and Physical AI**.

We do not sell generic video hours. We design and operate data campaigns for clearly defined robot tasks, including:

- synchronized multi-view and RGB-D demonstrations,
- hand, tool and object trajectories,
- systematic task and environment variation,
- failure and recovery sequences,
- quality assurance and acceptance criteria,
- rights, provenance and deletion documentation,
- export to customer schemas or compatible robotics-data formats.

## Initial focus

Flexible industrial manipulation: cables, connectors, hoses, clips, deformable components, machine tending and task recovery.

## Current stage

- business and financial model completed,
- ten-day feasibility sprint defined as the first paid product,
- initial robotics companies and ecosystem partners contacted,
- technical Robotics/Data Lead and first design partner currently being sought,
- no claim of a finished production platform or proven model uplift yet.

## We are looking for

1. **Robot teams** with a concrete real-world task and an identifiable data gap.
2. **System integrators and industrial partners** willing to define a small pilot task.
3. **Technical collaborators** in computer vision, 3D calibration, ROS 2, robot learning or teleoperation.
4. **Research and startup partners** who can provide honest technical validation, test access or introductions.
