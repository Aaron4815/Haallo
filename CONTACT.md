# Contact and Collaboration Paths

## Design partner

Relevant when your robotics team has:

- one concrete real-world task,
- a known or suspected data gap,
- a technical owner,
- an evaluation benchmark,
- willingness to scope a small paid feasibility engagement.

Suggested subject line:

`Design partner – [task] / [robot or model]`

Please include:

1. task description,
2. robot/model stack,
3. current failure mode,
4. existing data modalities,
5. test or KPI that defines success.

## Technical collaborator

Relevant for senior practitioners in:

- computer vision,
- multi-view/RGB-D calibration,
- ROS 2,
- robot learning,
- imitation learning or teleoperation,
- robotics data engineering.

Suggested subject line:

`Technical collaboration – [specialism]`

Include a project, publication, repository or system you personally built and the availability you can commit to a paid four-week test.

## Research or ecosystem partner

Relevant for:

- robotics labs,
- incubators,
- system integrators,
- industry networks,
- sensor and tooling partners,
- data and cloud infrastructure providers.

We are particularly interested in access to concrete tasks, evaluation infrastructure, qualified technical candidates and introductions to teams with real data bottlenecks.

## Financing and institutional review
