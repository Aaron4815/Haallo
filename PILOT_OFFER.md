# 10-Day Physical-AI Data Feasibility Sprint

## Purpose

The sprint determines whether an identified robotics task should be supported by human demonstrations, object-centric trajectories, teleoperation, robot action-state data or another data modality — before a customer funds a larger capture campaign.

## Intended customers

- robotics start-ups,
- robot manufacturers,
- system integrators,
- industrial R&D teams,
- Physical-AI and robot-learning teams.

The customer should already have a concrete robot task, technical owner and a measurable evaluation criterion.

## Scope

### 1. Task and failure analysis

- define the target task and operating boundary,
- break the task into observable phases,
- identify success, failure and recovery states,
- document safety and environment constraints.

### 2. Modality decision

Assess the need for:

- ego and external RGB video,
- RGB-D and calibrated multi-view capture,
- hand, tool and object trajectories,
- force/touch or instrumented fixtures,
- teleoperation or robot action-state logs,
- customer-provided model and benchmark data.

### 3. Data and variation specification

- episode schema,
- mandatory metadata,
- variation matrix,
- target mix of success/failure/recovery,
- preliminary volume estimate,
- data-quality and reject criteria.

### 4. Rights, privacy and provenance concept

- expected legal roles,
- participant and location requirements,
- purpose and permitted-use classes,
- retention and deletion concept,
- provenance documentation required for delivery.

### 5. Production and commercial plan

- capture setup and equipment plan,
- people, location and processing workflow,
- project risks and dependencies,
- estimated cost and schedule for a capture pilot,
- recommendation: proceed, redesign or stop.

## Deliverables

1. Task and modality specification
2. Variation matrix
3. Data schema
4. Quality and acceptance plan
5. Rights/provenance concept
6. Capture-pilot scope and budget
7. Executive go/no-go presentation

A small representative sample may be included only when task access, technical usefulness, safety and the legal basis are already clear.

## Commercial structure

- Duration: 10 working days after complete customer input and kick-off
- Introductory price for initial design partners: **EUR 4,900 net**
- Payment: 50% at order, 50% at delivery
- Follow-on project: optional; no obligation

## Exclusions

The sprint does not include:

- a production-scale training dataset,
- guaranteed model improvement,
- development of a complete robot model,
- continuous employee monitoring,
- unrestricted resale of raw video,
- hidden secondary use of participant or workplace data.

## Customer inputs required

- concrete task and business context,
- robot/model stack,
- existing data and known failure modes,
- technical owner and decision-maker,
- evaluation benchmark,
- access and safety constraints.

## First call

A useful first discussion takes 20 minutes and answers three questions:

1. What concrete task is blocked?
2. Which data gap is believed to be responsible?
3. What test would justify a larger data campaign?
