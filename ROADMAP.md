# Public Evidence Roadmap

This roadmap separates completed documentation/software work from physical, commercial and organizational evidence that still has to be produced.

## Gate 1 — Public technical specification

Status: **completed**

Evidence:

- task and failure/recovery taxonomy,
- capture protocol,
- episode JSON Schema and example,
- acceptance tests,
- data-card and quality-report templates,
- recorder and validator,
- automated CI,
- public demonstrator page,
- structured design-partner and technical-collaboration intake.

## Gate 2 — Physical demonstrator

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- physical cable/connector jig,
- two fixed camera views,
- calibration files and measured reprojection error,
- five equipment/dry-run episodes,
- timing report including software start offset and frame intervals,
- no privacy exposure.

## Gate 3 — First internal data sample

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- ten internal episodes,
- standard, variation, failure and recovery coverage,
- valid metadata and automated reports,
- full human quality/privacy review,
- aggregate quality report,
- re-capture of rejected mandatory variants.

## Gate 4 — External technical review

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- at least one qualified robotics practitioner reviews the task, capture design, timing claims, schema and human-to-robot limitations,
- feedback recorded,
- changes or rejected recommendations documented,
- no claim of external validation without explicit completion.

## Gate 5 — Customer problem proof

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- ten qualified discovery conversations,
- repeated and specific data bottleneck,
- one technical benchmark that could be influenced by new data,
- two written letters of intent or one paid feasibility sprint,
- no large hardware purchase before this gate.

## Gate 6 — Technical delivery ownership

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- qualified Robotics/Data Lead candidate,
- paid four-week test,
- references and proof of work checked,
- technical deliverables accepted,
- contract or strong letter of intent,
- confidentiality and IP assignment agreed before production access.

## Gate 7 — Company and financing readiness

Status: **open**

Required evidence:

- actual founder funds documented,
- founder availability and private sustainability documented,
- separation from the existing GbR resolved,
- legal entity and IP ownership structured,
- legal/privacy/security package reviewed,
- vendor quotations and insurance obtained,
- financing applied for only after the relevant customer/team gates.

## Current operating rule

Do not confuse completed documents with completed business evidence. The project moves to the next cost level only when the previous gate has measurable proof.
