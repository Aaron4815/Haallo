# Technical Collaboration / Senior Robotics & Data Lead

Physical AI Data Germany is looking for a technical collaborator who can turn a commercial task specification into a reproducible robotics-data pipeline.

This is not an immediate equity promise. The intended sequence is:

1. technical interview and portfolio review,
2. references,
3. paid four-week working test,
4. only then a longer-term lead, employment or co-founder discussion.

## Required capability

### Computer vision and 3D

- Python and production-oriented CV pipelines,
- OpenCV and/or PyTorch,
- camera intrinsics/extrinsics,
- RGB-D and multi-view calibration,
- coordinate frames, poses and synchronization,
- hand, tool and object tracking.

### Robotics and data

- ROS or ROS 2,
- sensor integration,
- robot action/state concepts,
- data schemas, versioning and quality checks,
- familiarity with imitation learning, teleoperation, robot learning, VLA or related systems,
- practical judgment on when human video is insufficient.

### Delivery behavior

- communicates trade-offs clearly,
- documents reproducible workflows,
- works with incomplete early-stage requirements,
- distinguishes a prototype from production readiness,
- does not overclaim model performance.

## Four-week paid working test

### Week 1 — Architecture and task schema

Deliver:

- capture architecture,
- data-flow diagram,
- preliminary bill of materials,
- task and episode schema,
- technical risks and decisions.

### Week 2 — Calibrated multi-view demonstrator

Deliver:

- synchronized test sequences,
- reproducible calibration procedure,
- timestamps and metadata,
- documented setup and known limitations.

### Week 3 — Tracking, segmentation and QA

Deliver:

- simple hand/object trajectory output,
- task-phase labels,
- success/failure/recovery representation,
- reject reasons and quality checks.

### Week 4 — Export and reproducibility

Deliver:

- customer-viewable sample,
- data card,
- export into an agreed robotics-data structure,
- complete reproducibility notes,
- recommendation for the first paid customer pilot.
